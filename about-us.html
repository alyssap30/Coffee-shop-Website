Head Chef

Your responsibility is to manage the kitchen operations manage the kitchen staff and give instructions to them. You must ensure the quality and consistency of all dishes served is outstanding. As head chef you will be responsible for menu planning, inventory management, maintaining food safety standards, and training chefs.

Key Skills

  • Cleaning Skills
  • Kitchen Health and Safety
  • Willingness to learn

Requirements

  • 1 year experience in a head chef/manager role (Essential)
  • Certificate in Food Hygiene
  • Certificate in Food Safety
  • Knife Licence
  • Bachelor Degree in Hospitality and Catering or a similar subject
